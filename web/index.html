Delivery matters: stock TTS reads Urdu. Tarannum reshapes the pitch contour into a raga — sustained vowels, portamento between notes, a held line-final note — while leaving the spectral envelope untouched, so the voice keeps its identity and the consonants stay intelligible.

How dispatch works

With a token this page calls workflow_dispatch directly. Without one it opens the Actions tab and copies your lyrics to the clipboard, so you paste them into the run form. Tokens never leave your browser — they go straight to api.github.com.

Adab & accuracy. Naat is vocal devotional tradition. Many scholars hold instrumental music impermissible — the default is a cappella. Machine synthesis can mispronounce sacred names; always have a human recite-check the lyrics and the audio before sharing.
